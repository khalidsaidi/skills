# Privacy policy

Effective 2026-11-05. This policy covers the `linkedin-sales-navigator` skill, published by Khalid Saidi.

## What the skill collects

Nothing. The skill is a set of instruction and note files with one local search script. It has no servers, analytics, tracking or accounts, and the publisher receives no data from your use of it.

## What the script does

`scripts/find_reference.py` searches the notes bundled with the skill. It runs on your device or in your agent's environment, reads only those files and makes no network requests.

## Prospect information

The prospecting workflow has your agent read people's LinkedIn profiles and may record their names, roles and related evidence in a workbook. The workbook is kept wherever your agent saves its work, never by the publisher. You control that record, and you are responsible for handling it lawfully and deleting it when you no longer need it.

## Other services

The AI product running the skill, and LinkedIn if your agent uses your LinkedIn account, handle your data under their own terms and privacy policies. The skill doesn't change those terms. Your prompts and anything the agent reads go to those services, not to the publisher.

## Questions

Open an issue at <https://github.com/khalidsaidi/skills/issues>. Issues are public, so don't include personal details.

Updates to this policy are published in this file, and its history is available in the GitHub repository.

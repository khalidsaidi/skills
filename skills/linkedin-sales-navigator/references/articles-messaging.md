## a106024 — Admin InMail and messaging controls
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a106024
Reviewed: full public article text, 2026-11-05; supplementary screenshots not visually reviewed.
Admin > Admin Settings > InMail and Messaging controls team sending, attachments and disclosure separately. Custom disclosure/unsubscribe text appears to recipients below sent InMail, not necessarily in the composing user’s draft. Dashboard-wide unsubscribe differs from one recipient declining one message. Change only intended scope and verify recipient-facing disclosure.

## a7146366 — AI introduction request
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a7146366
Reviewed: full article and three expanded FAQs.
Advanced/Plus default-enabled Ask for intro drafts a request to a mutual/TeamLink connection on LinkedIn or Sales Navigator. It uses product context, public information, common experience/education, job changes/promotions and posts from the past 90 days. Review/edit factual claims and relationship assumptions. The draft is not sent automatically; sending requires explicit authorization. Disclosures discuss inaccurate output, retention, human review/feedback and changeable availability.

Reviewed: full article; no tabs or collapsed sections.

## a105008 — Inbox routing
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a105008
Reviewed: full article; no tabs or collapsed sections.
Replies follow where the conversation began: Sales Navigator-initiated InMail replies go to Sales Navigator; LinkedIn-initiated conversations and unsolicited inbound messages go to LinkedIn. Recipients see Sales Navigator messages in their LinkedIn inbox. Replies to accepted connection invitations sent from Sales Navigator go to LinkedIn. Do not interpret a missing Sales Navigator thread as proof no reply arrived.

<a id="a101030"></a>
## a101030 — InMail credits
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a101030
Reviewed: full article and allocation table; no collapsed sections or tabs.
All plans allocate 50 credits on the first UTC day monthly, separate from billing/activation. Contact support if absent by day two. Maximum accumulation is 150 and unused credits last 90 days; extra credits cannot be bought. A response within 90 days refunds a credit, including Interested, Maybe later and Not interested auto-replies. Another unanswered InMail consumes another credit; an automatic reminder is sent within three days. Editing/deleting is limited to 60 minutes; deleting an unanswered message does not refund it. Credits do not transfer on cancellation or plan-type changes.

## a551012 — Invitation restrictions
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a551012
Reviewed: complete public article text, including all returned FAQ and platform sections, 2026-11-05.
Limits apply to Basic and Premium; no purchasable extra invitations. High volume, pending/ignored/spam invitations and prohibited automation can cause restrictions. Maximum network 30,000 first-degree connections. Withdrawal can prevent re-invitation for three weeks. Most restrictions lift within a week, but page also gives first-incident hours, repeated-incident days and outstanding-invite month waits; follow actual notice. Support does not override limits. Do not promise a fixed daily invitation quota or use tools to evade a restriction.

## a107018 — Send InMail
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a107018
Reviewed: complete Desktop and Mobile tabs.
Desktop Messaging → compose, or lead → Message; mobile Messages → compose → recipient. Locked profiles require unlocking. Subject limit 200 characters, body 1,900; desktop signature limit 150 included in body total. Desktop attachment icon/mobile Plus adds files. No native bulk sending or templates. Optional CRM logging needs Advanced Plus, admin/user enablement and an existing CRM lead. Verify recipient, final text and authorization before Send. Messages remain in the Sales Navigator inbox.

<a id="a101026"></a>
## a101026 — Sent/pending messages
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a101026
Reviewed: complete Desktop and Mobile tabs.
Desktop Messaging → All messages filter → Sent, Awaiting response, Accepted or Declined. Mobile Messages → Filter also offers pending, accepted, declined, archived, unread and sent. Deleting unanswered InMail does not restore credit; response within 90 days does.

## a105107 — Accept Extend invitation
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a105107
Reviewed: full article; no tabs or collapsed sections.
Opt in from invitation email or LinkedIn settings. Acceptance exposes connection paths to the organization's licensed sellers, who can request introductions; it does not itself send introductions. Participation remains reversible through settings. Verify the inviting organization and authorized data-sharing scope before opting in.

## a8017002 — Access Smart Links FAQ
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a8017002
Reviewed: full article and 13 expanded FAQs.
Advanced/Plus sellers, not admin-only seats. Contract-wide enablement, no per-user restriction. Disablement blocks sellers and viewers; disabling a seat makes its links inaccessible, and restoring same-contract access restores them. Multiple contracts have separate content. FAQ distinguishes formal license transfer from independently adding a new-contract license; verify transfer outcome. Mobile supports viewing and sharing, not creation. Anyone with link can view after identifying; nonmember email may be optional. Links are not normally indexed unless publicly posted; they are not private access controls. Company privacy-policy URL can appear on consent screen.

## a107031 — Assign TeamLink Extend
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a107031
Reviewed: full article; no tabs or collapsed sections.
Advanced/Plus desktop Admin Center → Users → available Extend licenses → email users → choose Extend and optional groups/permissions → Confirm. ## a8017003 — Create Smart Links FAQ
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a8017003
Reviewed: full article and all expanded FAQs.
Smart Links → New Smart Link: title, Add Files/Website, optional download checkbox, Create. InMail's Add Smart Link creates and saves one while composing. Modify changes title, content and permissions for everyone using existing URL. Maximum 15 files, 200 MB each (some smaller uploads can fail). Viewable: PDF (300 pages), Word, PowerPoint, PNG/JPEG/GIF (≤250 frames), MP4/QuickTime/WMV/AVI (≤30 minutes). Download-only: TIFF, Excel, Keynote, Pages, Numbers, ZIP, CSV, TXT. Website addition supports video links. Preview via More.
No password, expiry or per-viewer access restriction; forwarding cannot be prevented. Article says downloads cannot be prevented despite offering a download checkbox; do not treat that checkbox as a security control. Blocking rules affect viewer identities but do not conceal the author from recipients.

## a8000258 — List permission matrix
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a8000258

## a8013235 — Share Smart Links FAQ
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a8013235
Reviewed: full article and seven expanded FAQs.
Advanced/Plus: Copy Link and distribute via InMail, chat or email. Recipients need not subscribe. Content can be updated after sharing. ## a105126 — Shared-list FAQ
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a105126
Reviewed: full article and 17 expanded FAQs.
Only user-created lists; eligible sellers at the same company, not admin-only/reporting-only seats. Lists can remain visible after owner departure/seat transfer, with existing edit rights or via a copy; deleting the shared original removes access. Recipients can Remove from my lists without deleting the original. The FAQ says note additions do not generate alerts, unlike some older alert wording. Counts can differ because profile unlock access differs. SNAP can save to custom lists. Verify sharing eligibility, notes and view/edit rights before inviting collaborators.

## a8017005 — Smart Links analytics FAQ
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a8017005
Reviewed: full article and nine expanded FAQs.
Smart Links → Analytics shows engagement. Usage Reporting measures links created and total views. Advanced Plus admin enables CRM writeback under Exported to CRM → Log Smart Links. Writeback generally occurs once per viewing session, suppressing repeat sessions by same viewer within 60 minutes. Signed-in LinkedIn identification and prior matching requirements appear stricter than the article's later generic unmatched-activity explanation; verify unmatched behavior. Anonymous/self-entered identification does not ensure CRM writeback.
Salesforce fields: owner assigned, analytics URL in Comments, admin Created By/Last Modified By, event date Due Date, matched lead Name, Normal priority, Smart Link viewed record type, subject containing title, Completed when matched versus In Progress otherwise. Dynamics: Smart Link viewed type, same matched-status distinction, event Actual Start/Created date, owner's Created By, matched lead Regarding, title subject and analytics URL. Admin → Smart Links → Company privacy policy adds contract-level consent-page policy link.

## a107021 — TeamLink Extend overview
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a107021
Reviewed: full article; no tabs or collapsed sections.
Extend licenses add willing non-Sales-Navigator colleagues' connection paths. Recipients can decline or later change participation. The page says Extend overrides network privacy for eligible subscribers, while the standard TeamLink opt-out page says privacy settings can restrict visibility. Treat these as distinct participation contexts; inspect actual consent and current visibility rather than promising privacy behavior.

## a105152 — TeamLink Groups
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a105152
Reviewed: full article; no tabs or collapsed sections.
Only representative-purchased Advanced/Plus contracts. Support activates cross-dashboard/contract groups; a dashboard belongs to one group. Authorized contract owners must sign a data-sharing agreement, including when adding contracts. Processing is described as 48–72 hours after completed forms. Opt-outs apply across groups. Do not sign or submit legal agreements on the user's behalf without the required approval.

## a101027 — TeamLink overview
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a101027
Reviewed: full article; no tabs or collapsed sections.
Advanced/Plus TeamLink exposes participating colleagues' first-degree paths to prospects. Best path in filters include TeamLink, Executive TeamLink and TeamLink connections of. Extend adds participants without full selling licenses. Groups combines eligible representative-purchased contracts. A displayed connection supports an introduction request, not a claim of a strong personal relationship.

## a106114 — Work with shared lists
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a106114
Reviewed: full article and three expanded sections.
More/Actions → Make a copy creates an independently owned list. Save all leads/accounts or selected entities into another list separately. Remove from my lists removes one's access until reshared; distinguish copy, shared original and individually saved entities.

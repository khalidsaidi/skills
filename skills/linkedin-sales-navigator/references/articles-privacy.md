## a103026 — How data is used
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a103026
Reviewed: complete feature/data-use matrix.
Suggested accounts use LinkedIn views/messages; TeamLink exposes first-degree connection paths to teammates. Admins see usage counts/SSI, with LinkedIn activity opt-out. PlusCRM sync ingests/stores accounts/contacts daily; ROI relates activity to CRM closed deals. Team admin controls corporate data; leaving loses access to saved entities, credits and Sales Navigator inbox unless authorized transfer completed.

## a194057 — LinkedIn activity reporting opt-out
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a194057
Reviewed: full article.
Me → Settings → Privacy and data → LinkedIn.com activities off excludes LinkedIn actions from employer Usage/ROI reporting. Counts cover days, searches, profile views; public engagements are ROI-only. Specific searches or viewed identities are not shared. Does not disable Sales Navigator activity reporting. ROI is aggregate opportunity-level.

## a7440184 — Message Assist privacy
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a7440184
Reviewed: all FAQs.
Public LinkedIn profiles/posts, Bing-derived AccountIQ and seller/admin product entries inform drafts; article says no CRM data and not conversational AI. Inputs not used to train/fine-tune models without approval; optional feedback may allow related input/output use for product improvement. No cross-customer sharing stated. Personal LinkedIn training setting is separate. Model identified as GPT4o at publication, not guaranteed permanent. Human accuracy/bias review required; mitigations aren't a guarantee.

## a548106 — Profile discoverability
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a548106
Reviewed: complete public article text and returned platform/FAQ sections, 2026-11-05.
LinkedIn direct search does not allow email lookup. Public search-engine visibility, partner/off-LinkedIn discovery, email/phone matching and Page-owner export are separate settings. Email/phone discoverability controls do not block name/company matching in permitted external services. Settings > Visibility changes save automatically; inspect exact options on desktop/mobile and change only intended setting.

## a146129 — Smart Links data privacy and security
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a146129
Reviewed: full article with all three sections expanded.
Anyone receiving a forwarded link can access; deleting presentation revokes general access. Admin reporting counts creations/views but does not audit creation times, included content, recipients or modifications/deletions. LinkedIn acts as processor for customer content/insights; customer policy governs permitted use. Both members and nonmembers consent before viewing. Name/photo of author remain visible regardless of ordinary privacy settings. Content resides in Ambry, encrypted at rest/in transit, delivered via controlled APIs and expiring content tokens. No admin approved-content-only control; automated content scanning is not approval governance. Article expressly does not claim HIPAA/FINRA-specific compliance. Product undergoes LinkedIn security auditing/penetration testing; verify current assurance for procurement.

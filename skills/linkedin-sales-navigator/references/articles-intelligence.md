## a1511956 — Account Hub
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a1511956
Reviewed: full article; no tabs or collapsed sections.

All plans receive core account intelligence; intent/category signals need Advanced/Plus and opportunities need synced Plus. Filter all/starred/upcoming deals/intent/growth/risk, sort relevant columns and inspect recent changes. Connection Paths opens searches from first/second-degree or TeamLink counts.

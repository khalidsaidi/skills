## a122541 — Glossary
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a122541
Reviewed: full glossary table; no collapsed sections or tabs.
A lead is a person; an account is a company. Lists organize entities; saved searches preserve query criteria. Personas describe buyers through role and geography. Relationship Maps visualize stakeholders; Relationship Explorer discovers people and warm paths. TeamLink uses participating coworkers' connections. CRM Sync transfers supported activity/data; Embedded Profiles show information within CRM and require installation. Admin Center handles billing/licenses, while Admin Home guides setup. SSI measures brand, finding people, engaging with insights and relationships. Spotlights highlight signals such as recent job changes/posts. Connection degrees indicate network distance. Glossary entries can lag dedicated documentation; do not use its old profile-view window or simplified InMail definition to override current detailed rules.

## a104003 — Plans and features
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a104003
Reviewed: full comparison table and plan descriptions; no collapsed sections or tabs.
All three plans include advanced search, 50 InMail credits monthly per user, a stated 10,000 saved leads/accounts allowance, lists, alerts, profile/account pages, Relationship Maps and Premium benefits. Core is individual. Advanced adds CSV book-of-business upload, Smart Links, shared lists/searches, buyer intent, TeamLink/Extend and detailed usage reporting. Enterprise SSO/EDI is supported for Advanced purchased through Sales, and Advanced Plus. Advanced Plus adds CRM read/write workflows, embedded integrations, ROI reporting, sandbox support, CRM-role license assignment and Teams alerts. Supported CRM Sync systems: Salesforce, Dynamics 365, HubSpot and Oracle Sales. Freshworks has an Embedded Profile; do not equate this with full CRM Sync. ROI reporting requires enabled CRM Sync. Other integrations depend on configuration.

## a462366 — Referral program
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a462366
Reviewed: full article and 13 expanded FAQ sections.
Gift icon opens the referral hub. Up to 15 first-degree connections can receive unique links for two free months of Core. Eligibility excludes current Sales Navigator/Premium subscribers and members who used a LinkedIn free trial in the past 12 months. The offer can be redeemed only once per member; links are single-use. Preview the message, send only with authorization, track Joined status, or resend through More. An unused link can be redirected to another eligible recipient. Admin → Referrals can disable the program for a contract.

<a id="a1644013"></a>
## a1644013 — Usage restrictions
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a1644013
Reviewed: full article; no collapsed sections or tabs.
Unusual activity may temporarily restrict messaging, invitations, searches or profile views. Personalize messages, allow response time, invite appropriate known contacts and remove scraping extensions. Restrictions generally lift after 24 hours; contact support if longer or erroneous. Trials can have lower limits. Never promise a safe daily automation quota or bypass a restriction.

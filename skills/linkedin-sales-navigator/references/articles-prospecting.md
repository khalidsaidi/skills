## a1378574 — Account CSV upload
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a1378574
Reviewed: full article; no tabs or collapsed sections.
Advanced/Plus, accounts only—not lead uploads. Accounts → target list, or All saved accounts for a new list → Upload accounts → requirements/template → file → map headers → Finish. Existing total must stay within 1,000. Confidence scores 1–5 populate the list; score zero remains unmatched for report review. Check company identity after matching.

## a168061 — Boolean search syntax
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a168061
Review: full article.
Uppercase AND/OR/NOT, straight quotes for phrases, parentheses for grouping. Precedence quotes, parentheses, NOT, AND, OR. Whitespace implies AND; stop words may be ignored. No wildcard or official +/- operators. Company/title/keyword fields support Boolean; article says approximately15operators. Use compact logical queries and test live parser rather than assuming every filter accepts Boolean.

## a408255 — CSV requirements
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a408255
Reviewed: full article and requirements table; no tabs or collapsed sections.
Advanced/Plus account CSV: under 20 MB, at most 1,000 accounts, minimum account-name column. Use official company names rather than a URL in the name field; spelling/spacing and additional identifiers affect matching. Prefer the current template and correct delimiter/header mapping.

## a1376604 — CSV troubleshooting
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a1376604
Reviewed: full article and four expanded FAQs.
Queue load can change upload timing. Check first-row headers, consistent columns, correct mapping and 1,000-account capacity; rebuild malformed CSV if needed. Regional comma/semicolon separators can cause inconsistent-column errors; normalize/export correctly, using Excel Text to Columns where useful. Remove incorrect matches explicitly and review the match report; do not repeatedly upload unchanged bad data.

## a1452817 — Lead/account filter definitions
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a1452817
Reviewed: complete Lead filters and Account filters tables. Introductory UI illustration not separately inspected.
Lead filters cover current/past company, headcount/type/HQ; function/current-past title/seniority/tenure; person geography/industry/name/language/experience/groups/school; intent/category interest/company followers/profile and company-page viewers; relationship degrees, named connections/TeamLink, past customers/colleagues/shared experiences; job changes/posts; personas, lists, CRM and interactions. Distinguish person geography from employer HQ. Company-page viewers cover 90 days on Advanced/Plus, excluding privacy opt-outs; account intent describes 30 days. Jobs-changed/posting windows are 90/30 days. Account filters cover revenue, employee/department counts and 12-month growth, HQ, industry including children, followers, Fortune rank, jobs/recent activity/connections/intent, CRM and saved lists. Headcount derives from LinkedIn employees, not necessarily audited payroll. Pinning changes control placement rather than necessarily applying a value. Intent and CRM filters require their respective entitlements.

## a1422192 — Persona FAQ
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a1422192
Reviewed: full article and 16 expanded FAQs.
Maximum five personas including editable/deletable Director+ and CXO defaults. Attributes: function, seniority, standardized current title, geography; no keyword/nonstandard title support. Personas apply to lead search, account insights, homepage and Relationship Explorer, not recommendations or account search. Homepage matches are limited to saved accounts. Applying a persona replaces conflicting criteria; additional same-field values broaden matches. Editing an active persona needs refresh; existing saved searches retain old criteria. Persona growth compares current versus three-month-old headcount. Teams suggested-lead alerts use personas and their AND/OR logic, including any matching persona; changes usually take 24 hours. Other Sales Navigator/embedded alerts are not persona-filtered.

Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a102024

Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a101025

## a106027 — Search behavior and changes
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a106027
Reviewed: full article; no tabs or collapsed sections.
Keyword suggestions can map to title/geography/company/school; use the top search bar for keywords/Boolean. Two-pane filters support pins, live results and activity highlights. Deprecated: Sales Preferences, Posted Content and Became a Member; use saved searches, personas and Posted on LinkedIn as appropriate. People you interacted with combines viewed/contacted/saved activity; People in CRM replaces the older CRM-contacts name. Partner seniority is grouped under Owner; newer levels include experienced/entry-level manager and strategic. Account lists can scope current/past employers. Past Customer requires Advanced Plus; Executive TeamLink and Product Category Intent require Advanced/Plus. A suggested filter or default persona is not a user instruction.

Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a735730
Reviewed: full article; no tabs or collapsed sections.
Open Lead/Account filters or search by keyword/name; refine include/exclude criteria with live results. Suggestions are optional, not selected constraints. ## a105086 — Unsave entities
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a105086
Reviewed: full article and two expanded workflows.
Use saved-list selection → Unsave → confirm, or profile Saved → Unsave. The page warns unsaving an account also removes its saved leads. This differs from removing an entity from one custom list.

## a105150 — Mobile call logging overview
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a105150
Reviewed: full article and all expanded FAQs.
Mobile-only logs outgoing in-app number, time and duration, never call content. Details remain on device until user chooses save each time; saved logs appear as lead notes. Match requires connected member's voluntarily supplied number, or eligible synced CRM lead with number (legacy Enterprise wording). Saved leads alone do not reveal numbers. Disabling logging stops future access but does not delete prior saved records.

## a10856036 — Teams integration security
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a10856036
Review: full article and architecture descriptions; diagram image URLs located for visual review.
LinkedIn stores seat/Entra oid/tenant mapping, notifications and interaction logs; Teams stores notification content and conversation routing reference, not arbitrary CRM/M365 data. OAuth email/openid/offline_access/profile, no automatic identity match, no email/calendar/file access. LinkedIn sends adaptive card via authenticated Azure bot webhook; actions link to SalesNav. Azure AppService behind AppGateway, VNet outbound, ApplicationInsights/LogAnalytics; TLS1.2 and Azure256-bit AES service-managed keys per article. Uninstall marks integration routing row inactive and removes LinkedIn Teams-ID mapping; seat notification history follows normal retention. Verify current TrustCenter reports for assurance.
Supplementary media: image URLs returned HTTP 403; do not claim visual review.

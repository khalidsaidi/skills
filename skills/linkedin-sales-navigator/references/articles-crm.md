## a170004 — Contact creation
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a170004
Reviewed: full article and considerations; no collapsed sections or tabs.
Advanced Plus and CRM Sync required. Admin Settings → Exported to CRM → Create Contacts enables creation and optional existing CRM source values. Users choose More → Create CRM contact, authenticate, check possible matches, then complete the form. ## a163444 — CRM autosave rules
Source: https://www.linkedin.com/help/sales-navigator/answer/a163444
Reviewed: complete article and 13 expanded FAQs.
Advanced Plus admin enables autosave; individuals customize account/lead rules based on owned opportunities/accounts/records and account/owner teams. CRM → Sales Navigator only, not export. Lists count toward saved limits and have a 1,000-record cap, prioritized by modification and ownership/opportunity relevance. Changes process overnight, sometimes 48 hours. Users can add/remove accounts; CRM deletion does not necessarily remove saved entities. Lists survive disconnection but stop receiving additions. This newer FAQ says owner changes update lists daily, while older SMSC documentation says no automatic unsaving; verify actual CRM-specific behavior before assuming cleanup. Disabling all rules can empty lists; inspect implications for alerts and intent.

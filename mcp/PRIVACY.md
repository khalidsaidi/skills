# Privacy policy: Sales Navigator notes service

Effective 2026-11-05. This policy covers the hosted service used by the `linkedin-sales-navigator` plugin for ChatGPT and Codex, published by Khalid Saidi.

## What the service receives

When your AI product calls a tool, the service receives the tool's inputs: your search text, an article ID or a Boolean query. It may also receive standard request details such as your AI provider's IP address. Don't include personal or confidential information in those inputs.

## How it's used

Inputs are used only to return the result. The service has no accounts, doesn't connect to LinkedIn, doesn't write inputs to a database and doesn't use them for advertising or training.

## Retention

The publisher doesn't intentionally store inputs. The service runs on Vercel, which may keep short-lived operational request logs under [its privacy policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy).

## Other services

Your AI product, and LinkedIn if your agent uses your LinkedIn account, handle your data under their own terms and privacy policies. If you use the skill's prospecting workflow, your agent may record prospects' names, roles and related evidence wherever it saves its work. That record is yours to control and is never sent to this service unless your agent includes it in a tool input.

## Questions

Open an issue at <https://github.com/khalidsaidi/skills/issues>. Issues are public, so don't include personal details.
